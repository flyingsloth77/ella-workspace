# EU School Enrollment

## Trigger
Apply once children's declarations are accepted in the EC system.

## Steps

### 1. Get attestation
- Go to Sysper2
- Menu > Rights and Privileges > Request Administrative Certificates
- Choose: "Certificate for European Schools"
- Reference: https://digit.service-now.com/esc?id=kb_article&sysparm_article=KB0064233&topic_id=f78002adfbbaead80228f882beefdc17

### 2. Apply for eligibility check
- Go to: https://enrolment.eursc.eu/
- At the bottom of the page, select YES/NO and click the button

### 3. Apply for a position
- Window: 17 August 2026 – 21 August 2026
- Go to: https://enrolment.eursc.eu/

## Status
- Waiting for children's declarations to be accepted in EC system before starting
